Our Guides
Sulthan Pages takes its inspiration and direction from the luminous legacy of two great inheritors of the Prophetic path — Quthbuzzaman Sheikh Yusuf Sulthan, whose life and teachings continue to guide hearts even after his passing, and his true khalifa, Sheikh Abbas Faizy, who carries this living trust forward today.
They are not merely scholars or writers, but beacons of guidance, calling hearts to Allah through a complete Tawheed that transforms belief into life.
Quthbuzzaman Sheikh Yusuf Sulthan
A spiritual leader of rare depth and courage, Sheikh Yusuf Sulthan — known as Quthbuzzaman, the Pole of our Age — devoted his life to reviving the Ummah’s connection to Allah through pure Tawheed, deep love for the Prophet ﷺ, and active submission to Divine guidance in every sphere of life. His teachings remain a source of knowledge, spiritual clarity, and unshakable truth for seekers of Allah.
Sheikh Abbas Faizy
The true khalifa of Sheikh Yusuf Sulthan, Sheikh Abbas Faizy carries this luminous path forward. With unwavering dedication, he guides seekers to live by La ilaha illa Allah in its fullest meaning — not as mere belief, but as a reality that shapes the soul, the home, and the world. His words and example continue to awaken hearts and inspire a generation to walk the road to Allah.
