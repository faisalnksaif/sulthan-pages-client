My Mission is the Call to Tawheed
Quthubuzzaman Sheikh Yusuf Sulthan Shah Qadiri Chishti
The noble declaration La ilaha illallah, Muhammadur Rasulullah was the divine message upheld by every Prophet appointed to this world. Each one of them bore witness to this truth, and every bearer of this sacred message endured trials and tribulations along their path.
The core of my mission is the invitation to Tawheed — the oneness of Allah and the affirmation that Prophet Muhammed is His Messenger. The noble declaration "Lā ilāha illallāh, Muhammadur Rasūlullāh" was the divine message upheld by every Prophet appointed to this world. Each one of them bore witness to this truth, and every bearer of this sacred message endured trials and tribulations along their path.

It is through this lens that we understand the profound saying of the Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ):
"The most severely tested people are the Prophets, then the righteous, and then those who resemble them."
Indeed, our beloved Prophet (ﷺ), his companions, and Ghaus al-A‘zam all endured immense hardships — in their efforts to fulfil this mission. But they persevered, they prevailed.

The call to Tawheed, is not a slogan to be raised nor a banner for outward reform. It is the call to establish the Tawheed deep within the soul — to live in constant awareness of His presence, never allowing the heart to slip into heedlessness. True nearness to the Almighty is only attained through this continuous remembrance. When the heart is emptied of Tawheed, it becomes exposed and vulnerable — and that is when Iblis finds his path inward, stirring our thoughts, influencing our actions and tempting to follow corrupt desires. Our call, therefore, is not merely to speak of Tawheed, but to embody it — so that every inward state and outward deed is shaped by its light.

After me, it is the time of Imam Mahdi (عليه السلام). He too will face trials. Among his fiercest opponents will be the corrupt scholars of the time. However, those who have entered the spiritual chain (silsila) will, through the guidance of their Shaikhs, recognize the truth of Mahdi (عليه السلام) and will ultimately become his followers.

Those who oppose Mahdi (عليه السلام) are not the true scholars of Islam — just as today's corrupt scholars who oppose the pure message of Tawheed and its callers. This is because the hadith: "The scholars of my Ummah are like the Prophets of the Children of Israel", and similar hadiths that praise scholars, refer only to those who have attained ‘Ilm al-Ma‘rifah (knowledge of divine realities) and ‘Ilm al-Yaqīn (certainty in faith) — not those who limit themselves to outward rulings and legalities while neglecting the deeper work of purifying the heart and enlightening the soul.

The opposition of corrupt scholars, their power, and their wealth pose no threat to us. Our reliance is upon Allah alone. Had power, wealth, and empty scholarship been the ultimate measure of success, then Pharaoh, Qarun, Balaam, Ibn Sakha, and Jalut would have triumphed. But they did not. Instead, victory belonged to Musa (عليه السلام) who was raised in Pharaoh’s own palace, to Ghaus al-A‘zam who honored the Auliya, and to Talut who was chosen by Allah.

I did not begin this mission of calling to Tawheed on my own. I received divine permission to do so. And I now remind you: if ego-driven stubbornness, attachment to titles and status, entrapment within institutional frameworks, preoccupation with follower count, or fear of losing wealth and material comforts hold you back from embracing this sacred path — then know that you are heading toward utter failure in the sight of Allah.
